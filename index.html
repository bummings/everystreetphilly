why do this

The city of Philadelphia was established in 1682.

Philadelphia contains 3,937 streets, depending on who is counting, what qualifies as a street, and whether certain alleys, ramps, service roads, and suspicious asphalt appendages have successfully completed the necessary paperwork.

So ya run em where they are: broad avenues, dead-ends, industrial access, half-blocks, forgotten lanes, and streets that appear completely ordinary on the map but turn out to be a fence, a staircase, a loading dock, &c., &c.,

To pass through every street in Philadelphia is to move through an unusually broad cross-section of American life— rowhomes and skyscrapers, schoolyards and warehouses, shrines, murals, corner bars, corner shops, houses o worship, and memory all at once.
